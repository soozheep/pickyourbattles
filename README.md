# Pick Your Battles

**Sonic Remedy : Audiosensory Resonance** by Soozhee | Produced by Caelverum Music

Live site: [pickyourbattles.vercel.app](https://pickyourbattles.vercel.app)

## Assets to upload

Upload the following files from your Google Drive **09 Pick Your Battles** folder into the repo (exact names as shown):

### Root of `/assets/`
- `pyb-cover.PNG` (cover art)
- `09_Pick Your Battles.mp3` (or rename the audio file to this)

### `/assets/lyrics/`
- `pyb-01.png` through `pyb-10.png` (the 10 lyric cards — rename the Drive files `pyb-01` … `pyb-10` by adding `.png` if needed)

### `/assets/cards/`
- `pyb-card-01.png`
- `pyb-card-02.png`
- `pyb-card-03.png`
- `pyb-card-04.png`

Once the assets are uploaded, connect the repo to Vercel (or redeploy) and the site will go live at **pickyourbattles.vercel.app**.

## Features
- Full-screen lightbox on mobile/desktop for all lyric + teaching cards (× close + background tap)
- Mobile hamburger menu
- Shareable cards (native share / X / Facebook / LinkedIn / Email / Copy / Download)
- Custom audio player with progress
- Streaming links to Spotify, Apple Music & Amazon Music
- Professional luxe dark + gold theme matching the card aesthetic
